00 The finding

Nigeria's health facilities are ready in people and unready in power.

Of 2,804 facilities assessed across 12 states, 110 can run an electronic medical record today. The constraint is not staff willingness or data habits — both score near the top of the instrument. It is electricity, connectivity and backup: the things that keep a system running rather than the things that start it.

Anomaly worth a footnote

Jigawa holds 32 of the country's 110 ready facilities — more than any other state, and nearly a third of the national total — while scoring 1.0 out of 5 on Leadership & Governance, the floor of the instrument. Either facility-level readiness is being produced without state-level governance, or the governance indicator is not measuring what we think it measures. Both readings change the plan.

The hole in the plan

Leadership & Governance is the weakest domain nationally at 2.21 — and it carries zero costed items, because it is assessed at state level and the instrument only triggers remedies at facility level. The cheapest domain to fix is the one no line item touches.

04 One facility

The scorecard leads with the gate, not the average

A facility's average domain score does not decide its band. Two core domains gate it: if either falls at or below 2.9, the facility is Not ready regardless of how well it does elsewhere. So the average is a decoy, and it should not be the largest number on the page.

05 The language

Rules this direction runs on

Colour has one job

The three readiness bands are the only saturated hues on the page. Green cannot also be the brand, the button fill and the heading colour — the moment it is, green stops meaning "ready".

Each band carries a texture and a label as well as a hue: solid, dotted, hatched. Red↔green measures ΔE 7.0 under deuteranopia, so hue alone is not allowed to carry the meaning.

Magnitude is blue

Scores use a single-hue ordinal ramp, validated monotone and clear of the surface. Blue also carries every link and active state.

So the reader learns two rules and never needs a third: hue means band, darkness means score. The old five-colour maturity rainbow collided with the three-band scale and taught neither.

Prose in a humanist sans; every label, tick, code and column in monospace. Readings look like readings. Nothing is set in all-caps at display size — the shouting page titles are gone.

Structure, not decoration

No rounded cards, no drop shadows, no accent rails on tiles. Hierarchy comes from hairlines, vertical rhythm and figure size.

The page is an argument in five beats — finding, gap, states, cost, facility. The numbering is real sequence, not ornament. The old home page was a menu duplicating the sidebar; a front page should state the finding.
